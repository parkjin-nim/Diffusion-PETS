The forecasting-specific Diffusion-PETS backbone is trained on complete windows fully contained in the chronological training partition. During training, sample-wise period candidates are computed from the first 64 positions of each window, while the diffusion loss remains defined over the complete 128-step sequence. At inference, the same causal history-based period policy is reused, and CG64 adaptively attenuates the periodic transformation using only the observed history.
